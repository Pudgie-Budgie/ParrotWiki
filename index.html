Meet Your New Best Friend

Parrots are intelligent, social, and long-lived companions. Some are talented talkers, while others are beautiful singers. They can form strong bonds with humans and become wonderful little buddies. However, the more intelligent the parrot, the more human-like they are in their need for interaction. Without ample companionship, loneliness can lead to depression and behavioral issues like feather plucking. Choosing the right bird is a lifelong commitment. Browse our guide below to compare personality, lifespan, and care requirements. Whether you’re looking for a gentle beginner bird or a challenging lifelong partner, you’ll find it here.
